Caveats

  • All employee figures are paper valuations at the $83 IPO price. Day-one close was $97.17.
  • Options intrinsic value scales with max(0, share price − $2.91 avg strike); RSUs and founder stakes scale linearly. Wealth tier counts away from $83 are log-normally modeled from the Hill $83 baseline.
  • Vesting, staggered lock-ups (up to 10% sellable day one for non-founders), and taxes will reduce realised cash.
  • Distribution is highly skewed by tenure. Early low-strike options dominate value. Later hires hold smaller RSU grants.
  • Hill and Sacra estimates are model-based on public filings. Not official company figures.
  • Headcount is as of 30 September 2026. Current headcount may differ.
